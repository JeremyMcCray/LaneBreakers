Train the AI

Bots play thousands of matches against each other. The ones that score best on your chosen recipe survive and breed; the rest are discarded. Repeat, and the green line climbs. Nothing here is scripted — the AI works out how to play on its own.
This runs in your browser on one core. For serious training use node ai/train.js, which is many times faster.
