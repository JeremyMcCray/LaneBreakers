Train one recipe

Bots play matches against each other and the hand-coded bot; the ones that score best on the recipe survive and breed. Running the same recipe again continues where it left off.

Bakeoff — train several recipes, then make them fight

Each recipe gets the same budget, then the champions play a round robin against each other and your hand-coded bot. With more than one round the losers are dropped and the survivors are cross-bred — everyone inherits everyone else's champion.

Sweep — what does one number actually do?

Takes one recipe, varies exactly one weight across the values you give, trains each variant on the same budget and fights them. The clearest way to build intuition. Your recipes file is never modified.

Ladder — measured strength across a run

Takes checkpoints spread through a finished run and makes them all fight. Fitness is a proxy that can drift; this is the honest learning curve.

Recipe editor

A recipe is a table of wages. The bot learns to do whatever you pay it for, and nothing else. Weights left at zero are ignored. Prefer the differential terms — paying for "more last hits than my opponent" is a real goal, paying for "30 last hits" is not.
